help_request: The poster needs something for themselves, such as a fix, a recommendation, an explanation or confirmation of their own problem.
analysis: The post's question or position rests on a specific fact that the post itself states, such as a figure, quote, date or named company figure that someone else could check.
hot_take: The post asserts a position about how the world is, often phrased as a question, and offers nothing checkable to support it.
discussion_question: The post asks the community for experiences, opinions or predictions, with no personal problem to solve, no position taken and no stated fact to rest on.
